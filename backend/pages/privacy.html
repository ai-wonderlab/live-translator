Live Translator Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 4, 2026

Live Translator is designed to translate live conversations with as little data collection as possible. There are no accounts: the app never asks for your name, email, or any other personal information.

Microphone & speech

Your microphone is active only while you hold the talk button.

Speech recognition is handled by Apple, using on-device processing when available and Apple cloud processing when required by the platform. We never receive or store your audio.

Translation

The recognized text is sent to our translation service to return the translated result. Translated text is processed transiently and is not stored on our servers or used to identify you.

Purchases & credits

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. Your translation-time balance is stored on your device and in your personal iCloud key-value storage, so it follows your Apple ID across devices. Time is charged only for the seconds you hold the talk button. We do not operate our own purchase servers and never see your payment details.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data. We do not show ads. We do not track you across apps or websites.

Live Translator Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 8, 2026

Live Translator is designed to translate live conversations with as little data collection as possible. No account is required to use the app.

Microphone & speech

Your microphone is active only while you hold the talk button.

Speech recognition is handled by Apple, using on-device processing when available and Apple cloud processing when required by the platform. We never receive or store your audio.

Translation

The recognized text is sent to our translation service to return the translated result. Translated text is processed transiently and is not stored on our servers or used to identify you.

Optional account

You may optionally create an account (email/password, Sign in with Apple, or Google). If you do, we store your email address with our authentication provider (Supabase) solely to operate your account. You can delete your account at any time from the Profile screen in the app, which permanently removes this data.

Purchases & credits

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. Your translation-time balance is stored on your device and in your personal iCloud key-value storage. We do not operate our own purchase servers.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data. We do not show ads. We do not track you across apps or websites.
